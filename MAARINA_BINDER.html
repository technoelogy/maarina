What MAARINA Is

MAARINA is an open-source wearable SOS device for elderly people. One press calls 5 family contacts, sends GPS location via SMS, enables two-way voice. Passive fall detection. No subscription. Target price Rs2,499. Built on ESP32-S3 + 4G LTE.

MAARINA = Maa (mother) + Rina (Sanskrit: sacred debt owed to parents)
